SCREW SCHEDULE   (GRK RSS 5/16" x 4", star drive T-30)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every frame joint is a butt joint. Bore a 0.3438" (11/32") CLEARANCE
  hole through the outer member so the screw pulls the joint tight,
  then drive into the end grain of the piece butting into it.
  4.0" screw = 1.5" through the outer member + 2.5" of thread in the end.

  FRAME
    Header_House       through Joist_L, outer face x = -0.85"       2 screws
    Header_House       through Joist_R, outer face x = 45.48"       2 screws
    Header_Far         through Joist_L, outer face x = -0.85"       2 screws
    Header_Far         through Joist_R, outer face x = 45.48"       2 screws
    Interior joist 1   through Header_House, outer face y = 0.00"   2 screws
    Interior joist 1   through Header_Far, outer face y = -42.56"   2 screws
    Interior joist 2   through Header_House, outer face y = 0.00"   2 screws
    Interior joist 2   through Header_Far, outer face y = -42.56"   2 screws

      2 per joint end, at 1.00" and 4.50" above the bottom of the 2x6
      16 screws over 8 joint ends

  Heads land on the outer faces of the two long joists (behind the side
  trim), on the house face of the near header, and on the back face of
  the far header (behind the back trim). The RSS washer head seats ON
  the surface - counterbore the clearance hole where trim covers it.

  STAIR LEDGER
    Stair ledger       into rear post RL, outer face y = -43.73"    2 screws
    Stair ledger       into rear post RR, outer face y = -43.73"    2 screws

      2 per post, at 1.00" and 6.25" above the bottom of the 2x8
      4 screws over 2 posts

  These land in the SIDE grain of the 4x4, not end grain, so they
  hold in withdrawal as well as shear. Clearance-bore the 0.3438" hole
  through the 1.50" plank (and through the shim) only.  Penetration:
      post RL             shim 0.00"  ->  2.50" into the post (of 3.50")
      post RR             shim 0.29"  ->  2.21" into the post (of 3.50")

LAYOUT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Long joists (2x6, perpendicular to house): 2 @ 42.56", outer faces x = -0.85" and 45.48"
  Interior joists: 2 @ 14.94" o.c.
  Headers (parallel to house): 2 @ 43.33"
  Deck: 8 slats @ 5.25" wide x 0.9375" thick, 0.1875" gaps, parallel to house
  Stairs: 4 pre-made 4-step stringers @ 6.7500" rise, 8 treads, PVC risers
          tread tops at z = 12.02", 18.77", 25.52", 32.27"   (top tread is flush with the 39.02" deck)
          stringer bottom bearing at z = 5.27", stair projects 40.00" from the frame
  Trim: ON   Footings drawn: ON
  Hidden old model: platform-with-stairs

  NOTES:
   * Stringers are PRE-MADE (ProWood 4-step, Home Depot 279713): 4 steps at 6.75" rise / 10.00" run in 1.5" x 11.25" stock - the same geometry this design derives, so nothing is cut. CHECK ON DELIVERY: (1) the bottom riser against the other 3 - the design assumes the foot is dropped one tread thickness (0.9375"), and any difference is taken up in the paver landing (IRC allows only 3/8" variation between risers); (2) the top plumb face has to cover the 6.32" patch where it bears on the ledger, or the 2 hanging screws have nowhere to land.
   * Stringer feet bear at z = 5.27" - build the paver landing up to that height. First rise off the pavers is then a full 6.75", same as every other step.
   * Tread nosings project 0.12", 0.87", 0.87", 0.87" past their riser faces - the top tread butts the platform trim, so it gets the short one.
   * Stringer tops hang on a 1.50" x 7.25" ledger screwed across the two rear posts, top at z = 30.84". That drops the stringer one full rise, so the deck edge is the top step - no projecting landing nosing. The plumb face bears on 6.32" of the ledger.
   * Rear post RR sits 0.29" inboard of the ledger's bearing plane - pack that joint with a 0.29" shim so the 2 GRK screws pull against solid material instead of closing the gap and bowing the plank.
   * Deck surface is 39.02" above grade - over the 30" threshold, so a GUARD is required around the open sides (36" min in IRC), plus a handrail on the stair. Neither is modelled yet.
   * Posts are now ~29" tall on adjustable bases. At that height the frame needs lateral restraint - knee braces post-to-joist, or a ledger lagged to the house rim. A 4-post table this tall will rack otherwise.
